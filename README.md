# ADHDAI (first pass)

An AI ad agency that generates the worst ads in the world.

Inspirations: Coca Cola, [Agen+cy](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/meet-agency-r-m-z0sce/)

Brought to you by the geniuses behind [Hot Talk](https://www.hottalkllc.com), [Dumpster Casino](https://dumpster.casino/), and [Autonomous Art](https://autonomousart.org).

## What is ADHDAI

ADHDAI is a parody ad agency built to explore what happens when generative AI optimizes for everything *except* persuasion, brand safety, clarity, or taste.

It produces ads that are awkward, misaligned, overstimulating, under-explained, overconfident, and structurally wrong. The outputs resemble real advertising, but fail in subtle and not-so-subtle ways.

This is not a bug. It is the product.

## What ADHDAI Does

ADHDAI generates:
- Ads that misunderstand the product
- Ads that over-index on irrelevant features
- Ads with broken metaphors and tonal whiplash
- Ads that feel algorithmically optimized for attention, but not comprehension
- Ads that appear to follow best practices while violating their spirit

Formats may include:
- Taglines  
- Brand manifestos  
- Social ads  
- Landing page copy  
- Campaign concepts  
- Pitch decks (eventually)

## Why This Exists

Advertising is increasingly shaped by AI systems trained on historical persuasion artifacts and engagement metrics. This project treats “bad ads” as a diagnostic surface.

ADHDAI exists to:
- Satirize AI-driven marketing optimization
- Surface failure modes of generative persuasion
- Explore taste, judgment, and misalignment as design material
- Act as a conceptual stress test for automated creativity

If good ads persuade, bad ads reveal the system.

## What This Is Not

- Not an actual ad agency  
- Not a tool to improve conversion  
- Not a brand-safe product  
- Not a critique of any specific company (unless it accidentally is)

## How It Works (Roughly)

ADHDAI uses prompt engineering, constraint inversion, and deliberate misalignment to generate outputs that *look* professional but feel wrong.

Details will evolve. Some parts may be automated. Some may be curated. Some may remain intentionally opaque.

## Status

- Inconsistent quality
